Join us in this tech talk to discover new tools for creators to produce lifelike character motion at scale. We will present next-gen motion capture tools for high-quality animations and a unique generative library of endless animation variations. Together, these tools deliver scalable and realistic motion for immersive storytelling. The session is split into two parts: Technology Overview – Highlighting the core features of Moverse Capture and Motifs, and their integration into workflows. Hands-On Demonstration – Showcasing motion capture and animation scenarios across different engines and tools. The talk will conclude with a glimpse into emerging technologies, including advancements in view synthesis and real-world fidelity avatars, offering new possibilities for realism in virtual worlds.
